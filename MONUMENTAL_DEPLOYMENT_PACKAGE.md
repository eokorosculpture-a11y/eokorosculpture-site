## 1. ADAM / WITNESS
**98 x 20 x 20cm | Bronze | Edition 12 | 2004**

**Lightbox Description:**
First life-size male head cast. Africa rendered as outline across the face, Nigeria deliberately absent. Frontal gaze meets the viewer directly. Work explores diaspora, displacement, and the cartography of belonging. The cartographic absence becomes presence—a space defined by what remains unmapped.

**Images:** unnamed_1.jpg, unnamed_2.jpg

**SEO Meta:** Figurative bronze head, African identity, contemporary sculpture, cartographic art, diaspora

## 2. STOP AND FOCUS
**200cm | Bronze | Mould-family foundation**

**Lightbox Description:**
Two female figures face each other across an interval, hands extended in held space between them. The moment suspended—not touching, but present. Born from foundational mould. This configuration emphasises convergence, attention, the breath held before connection. Part of mould-family sharing formal origins with Three Graces and Promises III.

**Images:** StopAndFocus_Okoro_01.jpg, StopAndFocus_Okoro_02.jpg, StopAndFocus_Okoro_03.jpg, StopAndFocus_Okoro_04.jpg, Stop_and_Focus_1.jpg

**SEO Meta:** Bronze sculpture, figurative art, contemporary bronze, two figures, monumental sculpture
